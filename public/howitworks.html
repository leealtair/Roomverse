How Roomverse Works

Finding your perfect roommate has never been easier. Our simple 4-step process connects you with compatible people who share your lifestyle and preferences.

1

Create Your Profile

Sign up and tell us about yourself, your preferences, lifestyle habits, and what you're looking for in a roommate.

  • Complete your personal information
  • Set your budget and location preferences
  • Share your lifestyle habits and interests
  • Upload photos and verify your profile
2

Get AI-Powered Matches

Our advanced algorithm analyzes your preferences and finds the most compatible roommates in your area.

  • AI analyzes 50+ compatibility factors
  • Location-based matching in your preferred areas
  • Lifestyle and habit compatibility scoring
  • Real-time match updates
3

Connect & Chat

Start conversations with your matches through our secure in-app messaging system.

  • Secure and private messaging
  • Share photos and additional details
  • Video call integration
  • Safety features and reporting
4

Meet & Move In

Meet your potential roommates in person, discuss arrangements, and find your perfect living situation.

  • Safe meeting guidelines
  • Roommate agreement templates
  • Move-in coordination tools
  • Ongoing support and mediation
