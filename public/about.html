Our Story

Roomverse was born from a simple yet powerful observation: finding the right roommate shouldn't be a game of chance. Our founders, having experienced the struggles of roommate hunting firsthand, realized that traditional methods were outdated and ineffective.

In 2023, we set out to create India's first AI-powered roommate matching platform. By analyzing compatibility factors that matter most - from lifestyle habits to personality traits - we've helped thousands find their perfect living companions.

Today, Roomverse is trusted by students and professionals across 15+ major Indian cities, with a 95% compatibility success rate and thousands of happy roommate pairs.

Our Mission

To make quality housing accessible and affordable for every student and young professional in India by connecting them with compatible roommates through intelligent matching technology.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted platform for roommate matching, fostering a generation of young Indians who live in harmony and build lasting friendships through shared living experiences.
